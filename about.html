About Midrash Elyon

A burgeoning Kahal where men, women and children grow together in their Avodat Hakodesh

Our story

A home where families grow

Founded on a bedrock of warmth, growth and commitment by Rabbi David Haber, Midrash Elyon began with a handful of members seeking a higher plane in their spiritual journey. Within just a few months, a Shabbat minyan, designed for young men seeking an environment conducive to them, morphed into a full-scale Shul with multiple daily functions. Personal connection and individual, non-judgmental growth are the cornerstones of this budding Makom Torah and Teffilah.

Today, 6 years since its founding, Midrash Elyon is proud to be the home for nearly 300 young families in our community! With a deep relationship with the Rabbi and a commitment to strengthening themselves and their families the Kahal at Midrash Elyon is poised for even more growth in the coming years ahead.

Our Rabbi

Rabbi David Haber

The founder of Midrash Elyon, leads the Shul with a commitment to Torah, Tefillah, and the spiritual growth of its community. Coming from a family deeply dedicated to Torah scholarship and communal service, Rabbi Haber carries forward a legacy of making Torah meaningful and accessible to all. His approach reflects the importance of building genuine connections, inspiring personal growth, and creating a community where individuals and families can strengthen their relationship with Hashem. At Midrash Elyon, Rabbi Haber is dedicated to fostering a warm, welcoming environment where Torah learning, meaningful tefillah, and a strong sense of community come together to help every member grow.
